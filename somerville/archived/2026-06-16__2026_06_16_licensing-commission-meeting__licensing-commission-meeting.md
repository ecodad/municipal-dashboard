# City of Somerville Licensing Commission

- **Date:** Tuesday, June 16, 2026
- **Time:** 6:00 p.m.
- **Location:** City Council Chamber and Remote Participation

## Communications

1. Minutes of the Licensing Commission Regular Meeting of 5/19/26.

2. Communication from the ABCC ("Alcoholic Beverage and Control Commission") regarding the local licensing authority's ("LLA") option to temporarily allow its on premises licensees to sell alcoholic beverages for one additional hour not to exceed 3:00 a.m. as well as the ability for on-premises licensees located within the public alcoholic beverage consumption areas determined by the LLA to sell alcoholic beverages for off-premises consumption.

3. Commissioner Lynch, in his capacity as Chair, requests to temporarily extend the regular hours of operation for all alcoholic beverage and common victualer license holders to 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m., Monday - Saturday, and Sunday from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. from their current hours of operation, pending application, and approval from the City Clerk's office. This temporary extension of hours ends on July 31, 2026.

4. Commissioner Lynch, in his capacity as Chair, requests that duly licensed establishments may apply for a permit to operate until 3:00 a.m. during the period of June 16, 2026 through July 31, 2026.
   - Alcoholic beverage license holders are allowed to serve alcohol until 3:00AM with a closing time of 3:30AM.
   - Common victualer license holders are allowed to serve food until 3:00AM with a closing time of 3:30AM.
   - All terms and conditions of any special permit approved by the City Clerk's office shall apply and all rules and regulations of the Somerville Licensing Commission and the Commonwealth of Massachusetts remain in full force and effect.

5. Commissioner Lynch, in his capacity as Chair, requests to opt-out of designated public consumption areas as approved by the House Bill H5482: "Act Authorizing Municipalities to Opt-In to a Temporary Pilot to Extend the Hours of Liquor Licenses and to Allow for Public Consumption in Designated Districts in Summer 2026."

6. Commissioner Lynch, in his capacity as Chair, requests that the license holder Bindaas Concepts Inc d/b/a Rosebud, 381 Summer Street, AL22-000034 appear at the July meeting for a license update. The establishment has been closed for more than 30 days with no notification to the Somerville Licensing Commission as required.
