4. ORDERS, ORDINANCES, RESOLUTIONS AND MOTIONS OF MEMBERS
   1. Resolution
      - By Councilor McLaughlin
      - Recognizing the seriousness of myotonic dystrophy and expressing support for the designation of September 15, as "International Myotonic Dystrophy Awareness Day".
   2. Resolution
      - By Councilor Clingan
      - In support of striking Encore Boston Harbor workers.
   3. Resolution
      - By Councilor Clingan
      - That the Director of Engineering and the Commissioner of Public Works discuss with the Committee on Public Utilities and Public Works the City's current requirements for restoration of streets following utility and other permitted excavations, including whether the City has the authority and ability to require full-lane, expanded, or curb-to-curb restoration on streets that have passed the five-year street opening moratorium but remain in good condition.
   4. Resolution
      - By Councilor Clingan
      - That the Director of Engineering and Commissioner of Public Works discuss with the Committee on Public Utilities and Public Works the feasibility of establishing a Street Preservation Offset Fee or similar pavement-degradation fee, modeled on programs such as Cambridge's, to help offset the City's costs associated with utility and other excavations in public streets.
   5. Resolution
      - By Councilor Clingan
      - That the Director of Engineering, the Commissioner of Public Works, and the Chief Procurement Officer discuss with the Committee on Public Utilities and Public Works the City's current practices for establishing, monitoring, and enforcing construction timelines for municipal projects, including the use of project milestones, completion deadlines, contractor staffing levels, liquidated damages, and other tools to ensure that projects are completed in a timely manner.
   6. Resolution
      - By Councilor Clingan
      - That the Director of Engineering, the Commissioner of Public Works, and the Chief Procurement Officer explore best practices used by other municipalities and public agencies to encourage timely completion of public construction projects, including performance incentives, liquidated damages, milestone requirements, recovery schedules, and cost-plus-time or "A+B" bidding, and discuss which approaches could be appropriate for Somerville.
