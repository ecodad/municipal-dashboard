4. Review Draft Letter on MMBC Recommendation to Merge PTAC & SBAC (20 min)
   - The Review of Multiple-Member Bodies Committee (MMBC) was established earlier this year following the November 2025 passage of the Revised City Charter and the February 2026 passage of a City Council ordinance. The MMBC's charge is to – every 10 years – assess the function and relevance of the City of Somerville's multiple-member bodies (boards, commissions, committees, and working groups), and to recommend the combination, dissolution, and/or creation of such bodies. The current MMBC began their work in April 2026 and must deliver a report to the City Council no later than the first Council meeting this December.
   - At the July 8 meeting of the MMBC, three out of five members voted to recommend that PTAC be consolidated with the Somerville Bicycle Advisory Committee (SBAC) into a single Mobility Advisory Committee.
   - At this meeting, PTAC members will review a draft letter with feedback on this recommendation. If approved, it will be sent to the MMBC on behalf of PTAC and SBAC.

5. Review Proposed Changes to PTAC Bylaws (15 Minutes)
   - PTAC's bylaws are critical for guiding committee work and ensuring public transparency. Since our bylaws were last revised in April 2025, our committee's work has evolved and our needs have shifted. To fit our evolving needs, PTAC Officers have drafted a set of proposed changes to the bylaws governing member terms, subcommittees, Officer positions, and committee history.
   - At this meeting, PTAC Officers will present the proposed changes for discussion with the full committee.

6. Review Concept Design for Squires Bridge Replacement Project (40 Minutes)
   - MassDOT's Squires Bridge Replacement project aims to fix the deteriorating bridge along McGrath Highway between Poplar Street / Medford Street in Somerville and Third Street in Cambridge. MassDOT plans to take this opportunity to improve the road for people walking, biking, and taking transit, to facilitate better connections between neighborhoods (especially Brickbottom), and to make the area more accessible for people with disabilities. The project is being closely coordinated with the McGrath Boulevard project, as well as several other projects in the area.
   - On July 14, 2026, the Squires Bridge Replacement project team held a public information meeting to present a project overview and concept design.
   - At this meeting, Mobility staff will highlight key elements of the project and concept design for discussion among PTAC members. As we've done several times for the McGrath Boulevard project, we aim to send a comment letter to the Squires Bridge Replacement project team with our committee's feedback.
