# Somerville Pedestrian & Transit Advisory Committee (PTAC)

- **Date:** Thursday, July 23, 2026
- **Time:** 6:30–8:00 PM
- **Location:** Tufts University Administration Building, Suite 211, Conference Room 1, 167 Holland St, Somerville, MA 02144

## Agenda

1. Procedural Business (10 Minutes)

   - Vote on June draft minutes
   - Announcements

2. Public Comment (5 Minutes)

3. Subcommittee Updates (5 Minutes)
   - Community Events
   - Design Review
   - Special Projects

4. Review Draft Letter on the Ride Safe Act (15 min)
   - The Ride Safe Act (S.3077, An Act to Enhance the Safe Use of Micromobility Devices) is a bill in the Massachusetts State Senate introduced by Governor Healey. The bill stems from the recent work of the Micromobility Commission, which issued 16 recommendations to define and regulate different kinds of small electric vehicles based on their maximum speeds and engine power.
   - At this meeting, PTAC members will present a draft letter on the bill. If approved by the rest of the committee, the letter will be submitted to the Joint Committee on Transportation.
   - The Joint Committee is accepting written testimony on this bill until July 28. Find details on how to submit written testimony.

5. Deeper Dive: Somerville Ave Quick Build Safety Improvements Project (40 Minutes)
   - Last month, PTAC discussed the City of Somerville's recently released concept designs for the Somerville Avenue Quick Build Safety Improvements Project. At this meeting, the committee will do a deep dive on specific questions raised in the project including: bus stop spacing and consolidation, signal phasing options for key intersections, and push-button vs. automatic signals for pedestrian crossing.

6. City Update: Squires Bridge & East Washington Quick Build Projects (15 Minutes)
   - Mobility staff will present two updates:
     - MassDOT Squires Bridge Project: As a related project to the McGrath Boulevard transformation, MassDOT is preparing to replace the Squire's Bridge carrying McGrath over Somerville Ave Extension and MBTA tracks in East Somerville. The project team is expected to hold a public information meeting in the coming weeks.
     - East Washington Quick Build Project: As part of the Somerville Bicycle Network Plan, the City aims to increase safety and enhance mobility for people travelling on Washington Street between Tufts Street and the Boston city line. City staff are currently seeking public feedback on the project through an online survey, public input map, and upcoming street outreach events.
