## Agenda

3. Subcommittee Updates (5 Minutes)

4. Discuss Changes to PTAC Bylaws (35 Minutes)
   - Last month, we began reviewing proposed changes to the PTAC bylaws. Members raised several valuable considerations that merited further discussion. At this meeting, PTAC members will discuss these considerations in further detail with a focus on membership requirements and reappointment processes. The goal is to align on a conceptual direction for the updated bylaws so we can review and vote on a final draft at our October meeting.

5. Continue Review of Squires Bridge Replacement Project (35 Minutes)
   - MassDOT's Squires Bridge Replacement project aims to fix the deteriorating bridge along McGrath Highway between Poplar Street / Medford Street in Somerville and Third Street in Cambridge. MassDOT plans to take this opportunity to improve the road for people walking, biking, and taking transit, to facilitate better connections between neighborhoods (especially Brickbottom), and to make the area more accessible for people with disabilities. The project is being closely coordinated with the McGrath Boulevard project, as well as several other projects in the area.
   - PTAC began discussing this important project last month. At this meeting, we will perform a closer review of the matters raised in the Design Review Subcommittee's draft comment letter with a focus on the stairway connections to Brickbottom, crossing safety, transit connections, and environmental mitigation.
