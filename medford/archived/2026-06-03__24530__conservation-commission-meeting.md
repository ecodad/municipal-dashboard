# City of Medford Conservation Commission

- **Date:** June 3, 2026

## Agenda

I. Public Hearings

II. Old/New Business

1. Notice of Intent - DEP File #215-0248 – Main Street Intersection Improvements – Continued from May 20, 2026 – The Massachusetts Department of Transportation has filed a Notice of Intent regarding intersection improvements in along the intersections of Main Street and (Mystic Valley Parkway Ramps, South Street and Mystic Avenue). The proposed work includes curb line changes, sidewalk and curb ramp reconstruction and guardrail installation. The work will be conducted within the 200-foot Riverfront Area (Mystic River) and within the buffer zone to Bank, Bordering Vegetated Wetland, and Bordering Land Subject to Flooding. The proposed project is eligible to be reviewed as a Limited Project under 301 CMR 10.53(3)(f).

2. Request for Determination of Applicability – Invasive Species Removal – The Mystic River Watershed Association has filed a Request for Determination of Applicability for Invasive Species Removal in Torbert Macdonald Park, Riverbend Park and the Clippership Connector Path. Volunteers will be trained in person to identify and properly remove three invasive plant species (Oriental Bittersweet, Garlic Mustard, and Black Swallow-wort). The work will be conducted within the 200-foot Riverfront Area (Malden River), Bordering Land Subject to Flooding, and the buffer zone to Bank.

3. Request for Determination of Applicability – Vernal Pool Restoration – The Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation has filed a Request for Determination of Applicability for ecological restoration and installation of educational signage within buffer zones to multiple certified and uncertified vernal pools in the Lawrence Woods section of the Middlesex Fells Reservation in Medford, MA. The purpose of the project is to restore degraded buffer areas, discourage unauthorized foot traffic into sensitive resource areas, improve public awareness of vernal pool habitat, and close/restore unauthorized trails. The work will be conducted within the buffer zone of Bordering Vegetated Wetland and Bank, locally Regulated Vernal Pool and Isolated Wetland Buffer.

4. Request for Determination of Applicability – Vernal Pool Restoration – KAAT Services for Riverside Associates has filed a Request for Determination of Applicability for the demolition of an existing structure at 65 Riverside Avenue in Medford, MA. The structure will be removed down to the existing slab foundation and no earth disturbance will occur. The work will be conducted within the buffer zone of Bank, and the 200-ft Riverfront Area (Mystic River).
