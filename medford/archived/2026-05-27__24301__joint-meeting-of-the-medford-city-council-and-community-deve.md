# Medford Community Development Board

- **Date:** May 27, 2026
- **Time:** 6pm
- **Location:** Medford City Council Chamber, second floor of Medford City Hall, 85 George P. Hassett Drive, Medford, MA, and via Zoom Remote Videoconferencing

## Agenda

Joint public hearing with the Medford City Council relative to the following proposed amendments to the City of Medford Zoning Ordinance and Zoning Map:

1. Amend Section 94-2.1 (Division into Districts) and Section 94-9.0 (Special District Regulations) to add the Boston Avenue Neighborhood Corridor District (BANCD)

2. Amend Section 94-3.2 Table of Use Regulations (Table A) by incorporating the BANCD and to designate the uses permitted therein.

3. Amend Section 94-4.1 Table of Dimensional Requirements (Table B) by incorporating the BANCD and to state the dimensional requirements therein.

4. Amend Section 94-12 (Definitions) to amend and add various definitions.

5. Amending the Zoning Map to change the zoning district designation of various properties to the BANCD as shown on the maps entitled, "Boston Avenue Corridor: Upper Hillside" and "Boston Avenue Corridor: South" prepared by Innes Land Strategies Group and dated May 11, 2026.
