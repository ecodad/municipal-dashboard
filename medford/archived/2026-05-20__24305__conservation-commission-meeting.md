Notice of Intent - DEP File #215-0248 – Main Street Intersection Improvements – The Massachusetts Department of Transportation has filed a Notice of Intent regarding intersection improvements in along the intersections of Main Street and (Mystic Valley Parkway Ramps, South Street and Mystic Avenue). The proposed work includes curb line changes, sidewalk and curb ramp reconstruction and guardrail installation. The work will be conducted within the 200-foot Riverfront Area (Mystic River) and within the buffer zone to Bank, Bordering Vegetated Wetland, and Bordering Land Subject to Flooding. The proposed project is eligible to be reviewed as a Limited Project under 301 CMR 10.53(3)(f).
