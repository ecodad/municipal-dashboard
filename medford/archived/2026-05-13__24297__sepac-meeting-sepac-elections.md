## General Meeting/SEPAC Elections

1. Join us and VOTE for your Special Education Parent Advisory Council Representatives!

The Special Education Parent Advisory Council's purpose is to fulfill the directive stated in Chapter 71B of the Massachusetts General Laws which requires a school district to establish a Special Education Parent Advisory Council (SEPAC) and assigns both an advisory and a participatory function to the SEPAC.

SEPAC officers include but are not limited to:
- Co-Chair (1)
- Co-Chair (2)
- Secretary
- Treasurer
