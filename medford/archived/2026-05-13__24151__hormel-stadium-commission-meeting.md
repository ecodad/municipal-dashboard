### Meeting Agenda Items

- Mr. Peter Collery from Boston Glory is seeking permission to place a storage container at Hormel – will present some ideas on locations and containers to the Hormel Commission
- D1 Soccer is seeking the opportunity to discuss the following with the Hormel Commission:
  - I also wanted to follow up with a few questions regarding the memorandum for this season, specifically around the parking lot and sound policies.
  - Regarding the parking lot, we fully agree with the no tailgating policy. In past seasons, D-One has made it a priority to clean up thoroughly after each event, both inside the stadium and throughout the parking lot. We take pride in maintaining the space and ensuring it is left in good condition.
  - We do want to acknowledge that D-One is not the only group using the facility. There have been instances where we've arrived and found leftover trash or bottles from prior use, whether from other permit holders or individuals in the surrounding neighborhood. We also understand that the lot is public and may be used by local residents, which can sometimes include unrelated activity such as music being played.
  - In our experience, most attendees of D-One games park and proceed directly into the stadium, rather than gathering in the lot.
  - My second question relates to sound. We have attended a few Boston Glory games where amplified music, DJs, and even live band performances were present. While we are not looking to have excessively loud music like in previous years, we would like to understand what is permitted.
  - Specifically:
    - Is there a set decibel level that we should adhere to within the stadium?
    - Would it be acceptable for us to have controlled music on Saturdays during game hours, ending no later than 8:00 PM?
  - We want to ensure we are fully compliant while also creating a positive and engaging atmosphere for our fans.
