II. Old/New Business

1. Notice of Intent - DEP File #215-0248 – Main Street Intersection Improvements – Continued from June 3, 2026 – The Massachusetts Department of Transportation has filed a Notice of Intent regarding intersection improvements in along the intersections of Main Street and (Mystic Valley Parkway Ramps, South Street and Mystic Avenue). The proposed work includes curb line changes, sidewalk and curb ramp reconstruction and guardrail installation. The work will be conducted within the 200-foot Riverfront Area (Mystic River) and within the buffer zone to Bank, Bordering Vegetated Wetland, and Bordering Land Subject to Flooding. The proposed project is eligible to be reviewed as a Limited Project under 301 CMR 10.53(3)(f).

2. Notice of Intent - DEP File #215-0248 – 75 South Street – Kelly Morrissey has filed a Notice of Intent to raze an existing three-family dwelling and to construct a new three-family dwelling, porches, walkways, decks, stairs, permeable paver driveway, stone infiltration trench, grading and utilities. The work will be conducted within the 200-foot Riverfront Area (Mystic River), Bordering Land Subject to Flooding and the 100-foot Buffer Zone to Bank.
