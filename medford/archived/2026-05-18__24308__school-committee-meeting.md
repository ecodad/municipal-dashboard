2026-11 Offered by Member Graham and Member Olapade

WHEREAS, the Medford Public Schools' Project Transition Program serves students ages 18–22 with disabilities and is intended to prepare them for employment, independence, and full participation in adult life; and

WHEREAS, evidence-based transition practice shows that access to meaningful, community-based work experiences is a key predictor of successful post-school employment outcomes;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Medford School Committee requests that the Superintendent and Administration conduct a comprehensive assessment of the current Project Transition Program and recommend improvements to ensure alignment with best practices in transition services.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this assessment shall evaluate the program's effectiveness in:
   - Providing community-based, real-world work experiences, including pathways to paid, competitive employment
   - Supporting individualized student needs and transition planning aligned with student goals
   - Tracking data on placements, paid employment, and post-exit outcomes
   - Engaging students and families in planning and expectations

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Administration shall present its findings and recommendations, including any proposed programmatic, staffing, or resource changes, to the School Committee by December 2026

BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that the Medford School Committee affirms its commitment to ensuring that all students in the Project Transition Program leave with the skills and experience necessary to successfully enter the workforce and adult life.
